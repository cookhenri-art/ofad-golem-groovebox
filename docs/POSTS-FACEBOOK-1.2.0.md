## 🇬🇧 1. General post

🔊 OFAD GOLEM 1.2 is out — and this one is all about SOUND.

My free groovebox gets three new instruments built to hit hard:

🥁 **Kick Forge** — build your kick layer by layer
Stack up to 8 parts (punch, click, body, rumble, crunch, reverse bass…), 680 of them calculated in your kick's key, or drop your own samples (auto-tuned). Drag the fades and the timing right on the waveform, add a 9-module effects rack, pick a style (hardstyle, rawstyle, gabber, frenchcore, schranz, psy, rumble techno…) and hit Randomize.

🧪 **Good Doctor 2** — wavetable + granular + spectral hybrid synth
3 oscillators, 2 filters, 4 envelopes, 5 LFOs, a 10-effect rack with a 3-band up/down compressor, and a table editor to draw your own waves. 100 sounds, heavy on hard basses: gabber kicks, "suction" reverse bass, reese, hoover…

⚡ **Micro Tonnerre** — one universal synthesis for the whole drum kit
8 channels, each an oscillator + filtered noise, quick to tweak. 64 kits from classic to rave 96 and hard.

And everything that was already there: 41 instruments, 54 effects, 2,600+ sounds, 16 tracks, step and lock sequencers, scenes, song mode, MIDI track, phone remote. Windows, Mac, Android, iPhone / iPad, VST3 / AU.

## 🇬🇧 2. Kick Forge post (hard dance, techno, sound design)

🥁 Free kick builder: stack it, shape it, hear it — layer by layer.

**Kick Forge** is new in OFAD GOLEM 1.2:
✅ Up to **8 layers** per kick, each like a track: volume, transpose, stretch (pitch kept), offset, length, fades, filters, EQ, attack, distortion, mute / solo / polarity / reverse / lock
✅ **680 parts** calculated in your kick's key: punches (clean, techno, hard, industrial, distorted, zaag…), crunches, rumbles, clicks, tops, reverb tails, bodies, reverse bass — rumbles and reverse bass follow the tempo
✅ **Drop your own samples**: their pitch is detected and tuned to the key
✅ **Edit right on the waveform**: drag the fade-in / fade-out (length AND curve), the end, and slide the layer in time (snaps to 1/16)
✅ **9-module rack** in any order: EQ, M/S EQ, distortion, 8-point kick shaper, transients, convolution, finalizer (bass boost, mono bass, clipping), multiband, filters
✅ **14 styles** — Hardstyle, Rawstyle, Newstyle, Gabber, Frenchcore, Schranz, Industrial, Hard techno, Psy, Reverse bass, Rumble… — and **Randomize**, which keeps your locked layers
✅ The whole kick is rendered at maximum quality at every change: what you see is exactly what you hear, at almost zero CPU

Then sequence it in GOLEM with locks, scenes and the rest of your track.

👉 https://github.com/cookhenri-art/ofad-golem-groovebox/releases/tag/v1.2.0

Free, open source, Windows / Mac / Android / iOS, VST3 / AU. Show me your kicks 👇

## 🇬🇧 3. Good Doctor 2 post (synths, sound design, bass)

🧪 A free hybrid synth for hard basses (and everything else).

**Good Doctor 2**, new in OFAD GOLEM 1.2:
✅ **3 oscillators**, each in **Wavetable** (16 tables, 2 warps in a row out of 25), **Granular** or **Spectral** mode, unison up to 7, per-oscillator inversion and delay
✅ Sub-oscillator, noise, **2 filters** (18 types) in series or parallel
✅ **4 curved envelopes** (one for pitch: kicks!), **5 LFOs** including *pump* and *suction*, 32-slot matrix, 8 macros
✅ **10-effect rack** with a **3-band upward / downward compressor**
✅ **Table editor**: draw your waves, write a formula, or import a wavetable WAV
✅ **100 sounds**: gabber, frenchcore, hardstyle and rawstyle kicks, "suction" reverse bass, off-beat basses, reese, hoover, wobble, leads, grain pads, plucks…

👉 https://github.com/cookhenri-art/ofad-golem-groovebox/releases/tag/v1.2.0

Free and open source. Tell me which sound you'd like next 👇

## 🇬🇧 4. Micro Tonnerre post (drum machines, beatmaking)

⚡ One synthesis engine for the whole drum kit — free.

**Micro Tonnerre**, new in OFAD GOLEM 1.2:
✅ **8 channels**, all the same simple architecture: an oscillator (sine, triangle, saw, pitch modulation) + filtered noise (stereo option, "clapped" envelope for claps)
✅ Mix, EQ, distortion, choke groups and two sends per channel
✅ The channel's shape drawn live, and a mixing-desk view of the whole kit
✅ **64 kits** in 9 banks: classics, electro, techno, house, hip-hop, minimal, rave 96, hard, experimental
✅ Morph from one kit to another with GOLEM's scenes and crossfader, lock any setting on any step

👉 https://github.com/cookhenri-art/ofad-golem-groovebox/releases/tag/v1.2.0

Free and open source. Try a kit and tell me 👇

#drummachine #beatmaking #drumsynthesis #DAWless #groovebox
