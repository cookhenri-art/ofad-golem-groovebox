## 🇬🇧 1. General post

🎮 OFAD GOLEM 1.3 is out — with Gamer Lover, the sound of 8 and 16-bit game consoles.

My free groovebox gets a new instrument: all the classic game console chips in one place, with a CRT TV on the
panel that changes with the chip you pick.

👾 **8-bit**: square with 4 widths, stepped triangle, long or short noise
🎒 **Handheld**: the same square + a 4-bit wave table (8 waves)
🎹 **16-bit FM**: 4 operators, 8 algorithms, feedback, and the grainy converter of the time
🎻 **16-bit samples**: strings, choir, electric piano, brass… block-compressed like on the cartridge
🥁 **Console drums**: 16 pads, with coin, jump and explosion sounds
⏱️ **60 or 50 Hz tables**: the fast arpeggios (fake chords) of game music, pitch drops, delayed vibrato
📺 **Console output**: bits, sample rate, TV filter, 8-tap echo
95 factory sounds.

📱 And on phones, a trial: a pad-machine style screen — 8 menus, the TV, 8 knobs. Tell me if you want other
instruments like this.

Plus everything that was already there: 42 instruments, 54 effects, 2,700+ sounds, 16 tracks, step and lock
sequencers, scenes, song mode, MIDI track, phone remote. Windows, Mac, Android, iPhone / iPad, VST3 / AU.

👉 What's new in 1.3 + downloads: https://github.com/cookhenri-art/ofad-golem-groovebox/releases/tag/v1.3.0

## 🇬🇧 2. Chiptune post (chiptune, game music, retro)

👾 A free chiptune instrument with every classic console chip — and a CRT TV on the panel.

**Gamer Lover**, new in OFAD GOLEM 1.3:
✅ **8-bit chip**: square at 12.5 / 25 / 50 / 75 % (fixed or animated), stepped 16-level triangle, long or short noise, stepped volume
✅ **Handheld chip**: square + a 32-step **4-bit wave table** (sine, saw, organ, voice, bell, metal…)
✅ **16-bit FM**: 4 operators, 8 algorithms, feedback, per-operator ratio and level, converter grain
✅ **16-bit samples**: 8 short block-compressed samples, soft or hard playback
✅ **Console drums**: 16 pads (notes 36 to 51) — kicks, snares, hats, toms, coin, jump, explosion
✅ **Tables at frame rate (60 or 50 Hz)**: fast arpeggios, pitch drop, delayed vibrato, volume envelope
✅ **Console output**: 4 to 16 bits, full rate down to 11 kHz, Living room / Handheld / TV filter, 8-tap echo
✅ The **CRT TV** shows the wave, the FM operator diagram or the drum pads — tap the pads to hear them
✅ **95 factory sounds**: basses, leads, pads, keys, arpeggios, drum kits, effects (coin, laser, life lost…)

Then sequence it in GOLEM with steps, locks, scenes and the rest of your track.

👉 https://github.com/cookhenri-art/ofad-golem-groovebox/releases/tag/v1.3.0

Free, open source, Windows / Mac / Android / iOS, VST3 / AU. Show me your tunes 👇

📱 Trying a new screen for making music on the phone — pad-machine style.

In OFAD GOLEM 1.3, Gamer Lover gets its own phone screen:
✅ **8 menus** on the left (Play, Waves, FM, FM levels, Tables, Volume, Drums, Console)

## 🇬🇧 5. GOLEM + MPC / Force post (Akai groups, VST plugin groups)

🎛️ One free project, three ways to play: computer / phone, VST3 / AU plugin, and Akai MPC / Force.

**OFAD GOLEM** is my free, open-source groovebox: 42 instruments, 54 effects, 16 tracks, step and lock
sequencers, scenes, song mode. It runs on Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone / iPad, as an app or as a
VST3 / AU plugin in your DAW.

The same instruments and effects also run **as plugins on the standalone Akai MPC and Force**, with
touch screens designed for the machine (Q-Links on every page).

🆕 New in both: **Gamer Lover** — the sound of 8 and 16-bit game consoles in one instrument (square,
triangle, noise, 4-bit waves, 4-operator FM, compressed samples, console drums, fast arpeggios, period-correct
output with a TV filter). 95 factory sounds in GOLEM.
