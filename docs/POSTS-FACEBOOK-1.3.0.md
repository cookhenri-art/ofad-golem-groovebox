## 🇬🇧 1. General post

🎮❄️ OFAD GOLEM 1.3 is out — with two new instruments: Gamer Lover, the sound of 8 and 16-bit game consoles, and
Frost Fang, a psy synth that never sits still.

**Gamer Lover**: all the classic game console chips in one place, with a CRT TV on the panel that changes with the
chip you pick.

👾 **8-bit**: square with 4 widths, stepped triangle, long or short noise
🎒 **Handheld**: the same square + a 4-bit wave table (8 waves)
🎹 **16-bit FM**: 4 operators, 8 algorithms, feedback, and the grainy converter of the time
🎻 **16-bit samples**: strings, choir, electric piano, brass… block-compressed like on the cartridge
🥁 **Console drums**: 16 pads, with coin, jump and explosion sounds
⏱️ **60 or 50 Hz tables**: the fast arpeggios (fake chords) of game music, pitch drops, delayed vibrato
📺 **Console output**: bits, sample rate, TV filter, 8-tap echo
95 factory sounds.

❄️ **Frost Fang**: sounds that turn and morph non-stop.
🌀 **The ORB**: four sounds in the corners, mixed by a point that orbits on its own (Lorenz, spiral, star…), with
an opposite sub-orbit and elastic or gravity physics
〰️ **LFO sequencer**: 8 LFOs played by 4 lanes of 32 steps, 32 classic patterns (psy roll, trance gate, squelch…)
🔊 **Wavetables + FM**, your own WAV tables
🔔 **Extreme effects**: comb echo that follows the note, 12-stage phaser, reverb freeze
44 sounds: drones, morphing pads, glitch, metal, lasers, squelch sequences.

📱 And on phones, a trial: a pad-machine style screen — 8 menus, the TV, 8 knobs. Tell me if you want other
instruments like this.

Plus everything that was already there: 43 instruments, 54 effects, 2,800+ sounds, 16 tracks, step and lock
sequencers, scenes, song mode, MIDI track, phone remote. Windows, Mac, Android, iPhone / iPad, VST3 / AU.

🎒 Made for musicians on the move: the whole studio fits in your phone. No account, no subscription, no internet
needed. Plug in a MIDI keyboard or an external sequencer and play anywhere: on the train, at a friend's, on the road.

🎛️ Got an Akai MPC or Force? The GOLEM instruments and effects also run as plugins on the standalone MPC and Force,
with touch screens and Q-Links made for the machine (Gamer Lover included).

## 🇬🇧 2. Chiptune post (chiptune, game music, retro)

👾 A free chiptune instrument with every classic console chip — and a CRT TV on the panel.

**Gamer Lover**, new in OFAD GOLEM 1.3:
✅ **8-bit chip**: square at 12.5 / 25 / 50 / 75 % (fixed or animated), stepped 16-level triangle, long or short noise, stepped volume
✅ **Handheld chip**: square + a 32-step **4-bit wave table** (sine, saw, organ, voice, bell, metal…)
✅ **16-bit FM**: 4 operators, 8 algorithms, feedback, per-operator ratio and level, converter grain
✅ **16-bit samples**: 8 short block-compressed samples, soft or hard playback
✅ **Console drums**: 16 pads (notes 36 to 51) — kicks, snares, hats, toms, coin, jump, explosion
✅ **Tables at frame rate (60 or 50 Hz)**: fast arpeggios, pitch drop, delayed vibrato, volume envelope
✅ **Console output**: 4 to 16 bits, full rate down to 11 kHz, Living room / Handheld / TV filter, 8-tap echo
✅ The **CRT TV** shows the wave, the FM operator diagram or the drum pads — tap the pads to hear them
✅ **95 factory sounds**: basses, leads, pads, keys, arpeggios, drum kits, effects (coin, laser, life lost…)

Then sequence it in GOLEM with steps, locks, scenes and the rest of your track. It fits in your pocket too: the

## 🇬🇧 7. Frost Fang post (psytrance, goa, sound design, synths)

❄️ A free psy synth where the sound never sits still — Frost Fang, new in OFAD GOLEM 1.3.

The idea: four sounds in the corners of a pad, and a point that orbits between them. The sound morphs all the time,
and you can still grab the point with your finger.

🌀 **The ORB**: load factory sounds, your own saved sounds or the current sound into the 4 corners; the point circles on its own —
circle, figure eight, star, spiral, Lorenz, Rössler, chaos — with a sub-orbit going the other way, a third orb, and
rigid, elastic or gravity physics. 6 routes send the ORB straight to the sound.
〰️ **LFO sequencer**: 8 LFOs played by 4 lanes of 32 steps — every step plays a different LFO or a fixed level.
32 classic patterns: psy roll, trance gate, gallop, Euclidean, squelch, hitech, stutter…
🔊 **Two wavetable oscillators** (carrier + modulator), FM, phase distortion, fold, bitcrush, sub, colored noise;
load your own WAV wavetables.
🔔 **Effects made for extreme sounds**: comb echo that follows the note (tuned bells and metal), phaser up to 12
stages, flanger down to a few ms (lasers), reverb freeze, stereo width and Haas — all of them modulation destinations.
🎛️ 8 filter types, 16-slot matrix, arpeggiator, 4 macros.
🎧 44 factory sounds: psychedelic drones, morphing pads, alien textures, glitch, metal, lasers, risers, squelch
sequences and a few basses.

Then sequence it in GOLEM with steps, locks and scenes, next to 42 other instruments — on your computer or on your
phone, wherever you are.

👉 https://github.com/cookhenri-art/ofad-golem-groovebox/releases/tag/v1.3.0

Free, open source, Windows / Mac / Android / iOS, VST3 / AU. Show me the weirdest sound you get out of it 👇
